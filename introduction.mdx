## What is Player Forged?

Player Forged is a supplemental, at-home training app built around the real habits of professional baseball players. It provides:

- **7 training focuses** — Pitcher, Catcher, Infielder, Outfielder, Hitter, Base Running, and Speed & Conditioning
- **4 skill levels** — Beginner (ages 7–10), Developing (ages 11–13), Intermediate (ages 14–18), and Advanced (18+)
- **Phased drill programs** with clear milestones so you always know when to progress
- **Built-in video review** for Pitching and Hitting, with slow-motion playback and side-by-side comparison
- **Coach tools** for managing rosters, assigning non-practice-day training, and tracking progress

<Note>
Player Forged is a supplemental training tool. Always warm up before training, and consult a coach, athletic trainer, or physician before starting any new program — especially pitching arm-care work.
</Note>

## Get started in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your role">
    Open Player Forged and select **Athlete** or **Coach**. Enter your name — that's all you need to begin. No account creation, no password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your training focus and skill level">
    Select your position (e.g., Pitcher, Infielder, Hitter) and the skill tier that matches where you actually are — not just your age.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the phases">
    Work through Phase 1 at the recommended frequency. Complete the self-review checklist after each session and move to Phase 2 when you hit the milestone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Everything stays on your device

Player Forged stores all data locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server. Use the **Export** and **Import** buttons to share your training file with your coach, back up your data, or move to a new device.
