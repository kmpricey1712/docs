The sticker reward system turns consistent training into something you can see and collect. Every time you complete all the checklist items on a drill, it counts as a finished drill. Rack up five finished drills and you earn a brand-new collectible sticker that goes up on your Sticker Shelf — a permanent display that grows as you put in the work. The sticker system is designed for athletes and ties directly to their training history.

## How You Earn Stickers

The math is simple:

- **1 completed drill** = all checklist questions answered on one drill in one session
- **Every 5 completed drills** = 1 new sticker earned

Drills reset daily, so you build toward your next sticker across multiple sessions. There's no expiration — completed drills accumulate until you hit the next milestone and a sticker is awarded.

<Note>
  A drill only counts as completed when **all** checklist items are answered, not just some. See the [Self-Review Checklists](/training/self-review-checklists) page for details on how checklists work.
</Note>

## The Sticker Shelf

Your earned stickers are displayed on the **Sticker Shelf**, which sits above the Training section. The shelf gives you three things at a glance:

- **Progress bar** — shows how many drills you've completed out of the 5 needed for your next sticker
- **Total drills completed** — a running count of every drill you've ever finished
- **Earned stickers** — all the stickers you've collected so far, each displayed with the milestone number at which it was earned (e.g., "Milestone 3 — 15 drills")

<Tip>
  Check the progress bar before you start a session. If you're two drills away from your next sticker, you know exactly how much work earns you the reward today.
</Tip>

## Earning Notification

When you hit a milestone and earn a sticker, a **toast notification** appears on screen — a brief pop-up that shows the sticker design you just unlocked. It disappears on its own after a few seconds. The new sticker also appears immediately on your Sticker Shelf.

## Sticker Data and Athlete Profiles

Sticker progress is **per-athlete**. If you are training under a named athlete profile (selected from the Team tab), your completed drill count and earned stickers belong to that athlete and are stored with their data.

When an athlete's file is exported, sticker data travels with it — including total drills completed, which milestones have been reached, and which stickers have been earned. Coaches can see this history alongside checklist data to get a full picture of how consistently an athlete has been putting in reps.

<Accordion title="What happens if I train without an athlete selected?">
  Sticker progress still accumulates — completed drills are counted and stickers are awarded normally. However, the data is stored as a general session rather than being tied to a named athlete profile. If you later select an athlete profile, the unattributed progress is not retroactively merged. To make sure your sticker history is tied to your athlete file and visible to your coach, always select your profile before training.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can two athletes share a device without mixing up their sticker progress?">
  Yes. Each athlete's sticker data is stored separately under their own profile. As long as each person selects their own athlete profile from the Team tab before training, their completed drills, milestones, and sticker shelves remain completely independent.
</Accordion>
