After you finish a drill, a short self-review checklist at the bottom of the drill card asks you two targeted questions about what you just worked on. Questions are specific to the drill — for example, *"Did you step directly at your target every throw?"* or *"Did you keep your front shoulder closed through contact?"* — so your answers actually mean something rather than being generic checkboxes. Checklists are the mechanism that connects your reps on the field to your progress tracking and coach visibility inside Player Forged.

## How Checklists Work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete the drill">
    Run through the drill as described on the drill card. The checklist is waiting at the bottom of the card when you're done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer both questions honestly">
    Each question has a yes/no answer. Tap your response for each of the two items. There's no wrong answer — the point is honest self-reflection so you and your coach know where you actually are.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the checklist complete">
    Once both questions are answered, the checklist registers as complete. The drill card updates to show it's been finished for today.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Completing **all items** on a drill's checklist — both questions answered — is what counts as a completed drill. Answering only one question leaves the drill incomplete for that day.
</Note>

## Daily Reset

Checklists are tied to today's date. Each morning, every checklist resets automatically — your answers from yesterday are saved in your history, but the drill cards return to their unanswered state so you can log a fresh session. This means you can track progress day by day without old answers cluttering your current session.

<Tip>
  If you train in two sessions on the same day (for example, a morning throwing session and an evening hitting session), your morning checklist answers are preserved for that date. Completing the same drill's checklist a second time in the same day won't double-count toward your sticker progress — each drill counts once per day.
</Tip>

## Counting Toward Sticker Progress

Every drill whose checklist you fully complete counts as **1 completed drill** toward your sticker rewards. The connection is direct: answer both checklist questions on a drill, and that drill registers as done. Every 5 completed drills earns you a new sticker on your Sticker Shelf. No checklist completion means no sticker credit, so taking a moment to answer the two questions after each rep is the key to building your collection.

See the [Sticker Rewards](/training/sticker-rewards) page for full details on how the sticker system works.

## Athlete Data and Coach Visibility

<Accordion title="How do coaches see my checklist history?">
  If you are training as a named athlete — selected from the Team tab — your completed checklist data is bundled into your athlete export file. When a coach exports or reviews your file, they can see every drill's checklist responses with the date they were recorded.

  Coaches can also review checklist history live inside the app. In the **Team tab**, find the athlete's assignment panel and look for the checklist history section. It shows each drill, the date, and how the athlete answered each question, giving coaches a concrete record of self-reported mechanics over time.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is checklist data saved if no athlete is selected?">
  Yes — checklist completions are always saved locally and count toward sticker progress regardless of whether an athlete profile is active. However, if no athlete is selected, the data is stored as a general session and is not attributable to a specific athlete for coach review. To make sure your history is included in athlete exports and visible to your coach, always select your athlete profile before training.
</Accordion>

## What the Questions Look Like

Checklist questions are written to target the specific coaching cue the drill is designed to build. Here are a few examples across different drill types:

- *"Did you step directly at your target every throw?"*
- *"Did you keep your front shoulder closed until your hips fired?"*
- *"Did you feel your weight transfer fully onto your front foot at contact?"*
- *"Did you finish with your throwing arm fully extended toward the target?"*
- *"Were your feet set and balanced before every rep?"*

Because each question is tied to one drill's key mechanic, answering them takes less than ten seconds and gives you a useful moment of intentional reflection right after the movement is fresh in your body.
