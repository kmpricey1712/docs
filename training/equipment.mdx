Not every athlete has a full training setup at home, and that's completely fine. Player Forged lets you tell the app exactly what equipment you have available, and for anything a drill calls for that you don't own, the app automatically surfaces a practical substitute so you can still get the full benefit of the training. Your equipment preferences are saved per-athlete when an athlete profile is selected, or as a general setting when no athlete is active.

## Setting Up Your Equipment

The **Equipment bar** runs across the top of the Training section. It displays all available equipment types as clickable chips.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Equipment bar">
    Open the Training section. The Equipment bar appears as a row of labeled chips near the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark what you own">
    Click or tap any chip to toggle it on. A highlighted chip means you have that piece of equipment. Tap it again to mark it as unavailable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start training">
    That's it. Whenever a drill requires equipment you haven't marked as owned, the drill card will automatically display the substitute for that item inline. No extra steps needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Equipment preferences save automatically. If you have an athlete selected in the Team tab, the preference is stored with that athlete's profile. If no athlete is selected, it saves as a general app-wide setting.
</Note>

## Equipment Types and Substitutes

The table below lists every equipment type available in Player Forged along with the substitute the app will display when that item is not marked as owned.

| Equipment | Substitute shown when not owned |
|---|---|
| **Batting tee** | Have a partner hand-toss at the same height, or hold the ball in place at the target contact point yourself |
| **Weighted / plyo balls** | Stick to normal-ball warmup work; replace weighted-ball reps with extra long toss to build arm strength |
| **Pitching machine** | Use front toss or soft toss from a partner, positioned to match the delivery height the drill specifies |
| **Medicine ball** | Sub in a standard basketball, or load a backpack with books/weight to approximate the resistance |
| **Resistance band** | Have a partner stand behind you and hold your waist to provide manual resistance through the movement |
| **Agility ladder / cones** | Mark your grid with chalk lines, yard sticks, or lay shoes on the ground as footwork targets |
| **Wall or fence** | Use a garage door or any solid flat vertical surface that can take impact |
| **Barbell / dumbbells** | Perform the bodyweight version of the movement — squats, push-ups, single-leg work, and similar patterns |

<Tip>
  Even if you own a piece of equipment, you might not always have it handy. You can untoggle any chip at any time — the substitutes will appear immediately on every drill card that needs that item, so you can adapt on the fly without losing your session.
</Tip>

## How Substitutes Appear in Drills

When a drill requires equipment you don't have marked, a **substitute callout** appears directly on that drill's card, right below the equipment requirements line. It tells you exactly what to use instead and any adjustments to make to the drill setup. The rest of the drill instructions remain the same — the substitute just replaces the gear, not the movement.

<Accordion title="What if a drill requires multiple pieces of equipment I don't have?">
  The app will show a substitute for each missing item separately. If a drill needs both a batting tee and weighted balls, and you have neither, you'll see two inline substitute callouts — one for the tee and one for the balls — so you can set up each workaround before you start the drill.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I mix owned equipment with substitutes?">
  Yes. Your equipment settings are independent per item. You can own a batting tee but not weighted balls, and the app will only show the sub for the balls. Toggle each chip to reflect exactly what's available to you on any given day.
</Accordion>

## Equipment Saves Per-Athlete

When you select an athlete from the Team tab before training, all equipment preferences you set are stored with that athlete's profile. This means two athletes on the same device can have completely different equipment setups — one with a full cage, one training in a backyard — and each gets the right substitutes for their situation automatically when their profile is active.

If no athlete is selected, the equipment preferences are saved as a general setting that applies to any unattributed training session.
