The Training tab is where your Player Forged program lives. You select a **Training Focus** (your position or goal) and a **Skill Level**, and the app serves up a structured, phased drill program built specifically for that combination. There are no fixed calendar dates to follow — instead, each drill carries a weekly frequency target, and you advance to the next phase when you hit your milestone, not when the calendar says so.

## How to get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Training tab">
    Tap **Training** in the bottom navigation bar. If a coach has assigned training for you, a banner at the top of the screen will show today's assignment — tap it to jump directly to the right focus and level.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Training Focus">
    Select the position or goal that matches what you want to work on. You can switch focuses at any time without losing your progress in another focus.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your Skill Level">
    Choose the tier that honestly reflects where you are right now. You can always change this later if the program feels too easy or too demanding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Always start with the Warm-Up Block">
    Before every single session, complete the **Warm-Up Block** shown at the top of your program. It takes only a few minutes and requires nothing but your body.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work through your phase drills">
    Each drill lists its weekly frequency (for example, "3×/week"). Do each drill at that frequency and track your completions. When you meet your phase milestone, you'll advance to the next phase.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The Warm-Up Block

<Note>
Always complete the Warm-Up Block before starting any drill — no exceptions. Skipping it increases your risk of strain, especially during throwing and sprint work.
</Note>

Every training focus at every skill level begins with the same **Warm-Up Block**. It is:

- **Bodyweight only** — no equipment needed, ever
- **Position-agnostic** — the same block appears across all seven focuses
- **Short** — typically 5–8 minutes of dynamic movement, arm circles, hip openers, and light activation drills
- **Mandatory** — the app places it at the very top of every session view as a reminder

The warm-up block does not count toward your phase milestone. Think of it as the ignition before every rep.

## The day banner

At the top of the Training tab you may see a **day banner** — a highlighted strip that shows today's calendar assignment. This appears when:

- Your coach has assigned a specific training focus and level for today, **or**
- You have a recurring training day scheduled that falls on today's date

Tapping the banner auto-navigates you to the correct Training Focus and Skill Level combination your coach selected. You don't need to manually find the right program — the app takes you straight there.

<Tip>
Even on days without a banner, you can open any focus and keep building reps. The banner is a guide, not a gate.
</Tip>

## The 7 Training Focuses

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Pitcher" icon="baseball">
    Arm care, mechanical drills, pitch sequencing, and command work. Includes the **Video Review tool** so you can film and compare your delivery frame by frame.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Catcher" icon="shield">
    Blocking, framing, pop-time work, and game-management fundamentals. Built around the unique physical demands of playing behind the plate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Infielder" icon="arrows-left-right">
    Ground ball footwork, range extension, double-play feeds, and throwing mechanics for corner and up-the-middle positions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Outfielder" icon="expand">
    First-step reads, drop-step technique, routes to the ball, and outfield throwing with crow-hop mechanics.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hitter" icon="baseball-bat-ball">
    Swing mechanics, tee work, soft-toss progressions, and pitch-recognition drills. Includes the **Video Review tool** for frame-by-frame swing analysis.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Base Running" icon="person-running">
    Lead mechanics, jump reads, rounding angles, and steal-attempt timing. No base? The program uses cones or visual markers as substitutes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Speed & Conditioning" icon="bolt">
    Linear speed, lateral quickness, first-step explosiveness, and baseball-specific conditioning circuits. Complements every other focus.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Video Review: Pitcher & Hitter only

<Note>
The Video Review tool is available exclusively within the **Pitcher** and **Hitter** focuses. It lets you record a rep, play it back in slow motion, and compare it side by side against a reference. See [Video Review](/training/video-review) for full instructions.
</Note>

## Switching focuses

You can hold progress in multiple focuses at the same time. Switching from Infielder to Speed & Conditioning, for example, does not reset either program. Your phase, milestone completions, and drill history remain intact in each focus independently.

<Tip>
Many players run a primary focus (their position) and a secondary focus (Speed & Conditioning or Base Running) simultaneously. Just manage your total weekly workload so you're not overloading recovery.
</Tip>
