The Practice Calendar tab gives you a full-week view of your team's training schedule, letting you mark which days have team practices, which are for solo work, and which are rest days. More than a simple schedule, the calendar is a coordination layer between your team sessions and your athletes' home training — when you tag the Training Focuses covered in a team practice, the app automatically surfaces the *uncovered* focuses to athletes for their solo days. Everyone trains smarter, not more.

## The 7-day week grid

The calendar displays a **Monday through Sunday grid**, with one column for each day of the week. Each day has a day type selector at the top and, for certain day types, content below it for tagging focuses.

### Day types

Each day can be set to one of four states:

| Day type | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Not set** | No training designation for this day — appears neutral in the grid |
| **Team practice** | A scheduled team session; you can tag which Training Focuses will be covered |
| **Solo practice** | A home training day; athletes see which focuses they should target |
| **Rest day** | A designated rest and recovery day; no training targets are shown |

## Setting up a team practice day (Coach view)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to Coach view">
    At the top of the Practice Calendar tab, use the **role toggle** to select **Coach view**. This perspective lets you edit day types and tag focuses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a day">
    Tap the day type selector for the day you want to configure and choose **Team practice**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tag Training Focuses">
    Once a day is marked as Team practice, a row of focus chips appears beneath it. Tap each chip for the Training Focuses your practice will cover — for example, Pitching Mechanics, Base Running, or Fielding. You can tag as many focuses as apply to that session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Tag focuses accurately and completely. The app uses these tags to calculate which focuses are *not* covered this week — that gap list is what drives the suggested training chips in each athlete's assignment panel and what athletes see on their solo days.
</Tip>

## What athletes see (Player view)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to Player view">
    Toggle to **Player view** using the role toggle at the top of the Practice Calendar tab. This shows you exactly what your athletes see when they open the calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review solo day content">
    On days marked as **Solo practice**, the calendar shows athletes a list of Training Focuses that are **not** covered by any team practice this week. These are the focuses the athlete should prioritize in their home training to fill the week's gaps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This design means athletes never have to guess what to work on independently. They see a targeted list rather than the entire focus library, keeping solo sessions focused and efficient.

<Note>
  Team practice days appear on athletes' calendars as "Team practice" without revealing the tagged focuses or any internal coaching notes — athletes just see the day type, not the behind-the-scenes planning.
</Note>

## Today's training banner

Whatever day type is assigned to today's date automatically surfaces as a **banner at the top of the Training tab** for both coaches and athletes. If today is a team practice day, the banner indicates that. If it's a solo day, the banner shows the recommended focuses. If it's a rest day, the banner prompts rest and recovery. This keeps the day's intent front and center without requiring anyone to navigate to the calendar.

## Saving and portability

The Practice Calendar state is **saved locally** on this device. The calendar also travels with the team export file — when athletes import your file, they receive your current week's schedule along with all team data. This ensures everyone is working from the same weekly plan, even if they set up the app mid-week.

<Warning>
  The calendar shows the current week only and does not currently support scheduling future weeks in advance. Update the calendar at the start of each week to keep solo training guidance accurate for your athletes.
</Warning>

## Coach view vs. Player view

The **role toggle** on the Practice Calendar tab is a preview tool — it switches the display between the coach's editing perspective and the player's read-only perspective. Toggling to Player view does not change your role in the app; it simply lets you see what your athletes will see so you can confirm the calendar communicates clearly before distributing your team export.
