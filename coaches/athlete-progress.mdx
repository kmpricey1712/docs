Player Forged gives you a detailed window into each athlete's training history so you can spot consistency, identify gaps, and recognize effort — all from the Team tab. Progress data comes from each athlete's personal training file, which they can export from their own device and share with you. Once you import those files, each athlete's panel in the Team tab populates with their complete self-review history.

## Importing an athlete's training file

Athlete progress data lives on the athlete's device and travels as a JSON file. To bring it into your coaching view, you'll import it through the data bar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the data bar">
    At the top of the Team tab, find the data management bar. It contains the import and export controls for your team's data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Import">
    Tap the **Import** button in the data bar. A file picker will open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select one or more athlete JSON files">
    Navigate to the athlete JSON file(s) you've received from your players. You can select multiple files at once to import several athletes in a single step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the import">
    After selecting the files, confirm the import. Each athlete's progress panel in the Team tab will update with their history immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Athletes generate their own JSON files by exporting from their Training tab. Ask players to share their file with you at the start of each week or after a training block so your view stays current.
</Note>

## Reading the self-review checklist history

Once an athlete's file is imported, expand their panel in the Team tab to see their self-review history. The history is organized by entry, where each entry represents a single drill session the athlete logged.

### Recent entries (within the past ~3 weeks)

Recent entries are displayed in full detail:

- **Date** — The exact date the drill was logged
- **Drill name** — The name of the specific drill performed
- **Training Focus** — The focus category the drill belongs to
- **Skill Level** — The level at which the drill was performed
- **Self-review checklist** — Each checklist item from that drill shown individually, with a checkmark if the athlete marked it complete or an empty box if they skipped it

This level of detail lets you see not just whether an athlete trained, but *how* they trained — which cues and technique points they paid attention to.

### Older entries (beyond ~3 weeks)

To keep the view manageable, entries older than approximately three weeks are **summarized by week**. Each weekly summary shows:

- The week date range
- The number of checklist items the athlete checked off vs. the total number of items across all drills that week (for example, "18 / 24 checked")

This gives you a quick measure of consistency and thoroughness over a longer period without cluttering the panel.

<Tip>
  A high checked-to-total ratio over several weeks is a strong signal that an athlete is engaging seriously with the self-coaching process, not just going through the motions.
</Tip>

## Sticker shelf and drill completions

In the **Training tab**, the sticker shelf tracks each athlete's drill completions and earned stickers. When you set **"Training as"** to a specific athlete, the sticker shelf updates to show that athlete's individual data — their completed drills and the stickers they've earned for hitting training milestones.

This is useful for recognizing effort in team settings: pull up an athlete's shelf during a huddle to celebrate their progress, or use it to identify athletes who may need encouragement to stay consistent.

<Note>
  The sticker shelf and "Training as" selector are available in the Training tab. Switching the "Training as" value does not affect your own coaching data — it simply changes whose progress is displayed in the shelf.
</Note>
