Player Forged isn't just a training tool — it's your team's app. The **Team branding** panel in the Team tab lets you replace the app's default look with your team's colors, name, and logo. Changes apply the moment you make them, and every customization travels with the team export file so athletes see your team's identity the instant they import your data.

## Accessing the Team branding panel

Open the **Team tab** and scroll to the **Team branding** section. All branding controls are grouped here. You must be using the app in the **Coach** role to see and edit this panel.

## Branding options

### Team name

Enter your team's name in the **Team name** field. This name replaces the default app label and appears in the **app header** throughout the experience — both in your coaching view and in your athletes' player view after they import your team file.

### Primary color

The **Primary color** picker controls the green grass color used throughout the app's interface — field backgrounds, active states, and other primary UI elements. Click the color swatch or enter a hex value to set your team's primary color.

<Tip>
  Pick a color that works well as a large background element. Rich, saturated tones tend to look great on the field-style UI.
</Tip>

### Accent color

The **Accent color** picker controls the orange/clay color used for highlights, buttons, and emphasis elements — the infield-dirt tone in the default theme. Set this to your team's secondary color for a cohesive look.

### Logo

Tap the **Logo** upload area and select an image file from your device. Supported formats include PNG, JPG, and SVG. The image is stored as base64 data in local storage, so it doesn't require an internet connection or external hosting — everything lives in the app's local data.

<Note>
  Logos are embedded directly in the app data, which means they travel seamlessly with your team export file. Athletes who import your file will see your logo without needing to download anything separately.
</Note>

## Text color auto-adjustment

You don't need to worry about text legibility when choosing your colors. Player Forged automatically adjusts text colors — switching between light and dark as needed — to maintain readability against your chosen primary and accent colors. This works across all UI elements that use your custom palette.

## Applying and previewing changes

Branding changes are applied **instantly** — there's no save button to press. As soon as you update a field or pick a color, the app refreshes to reflect your change. You can preview exactly how everything will look while you're still in the Team tab.

## Resetting to the default look

If you want to remove your customizations and return to Player Forged's default green and orange theme, tap **Reset to default look** at the bottom of the Team branding panel. This removes your team name override, clears both custom colors, and removes the uploaded logo.

<Warning>
  Resetting to the default look cannot be undone. Your custom color values and logo will be cleared from the app's local storage. Re-upload your logo and re-enter your colors if you want to restore your branding after a reset.
</Warning>

## Branding and the team export file

All branding settings — team name, primary color, accent color, and logo — are bundled into your **team export file**. When athletes import your file, the app immediately applies your team's branding to their experience. This ensures your whole roster sees a consistent, team-branded interface regardless of which device they're using.
