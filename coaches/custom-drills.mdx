Player Forged comes loaded with training content, but every team has its own system — specific drills, coaching cues, and progressions that reflect the way you teach the game. The **Custom training content** panel in the Team tab lets you author your own drills and slot them into any Training Focus + Skill Level combination in the app. Once added, they appear for every athlete using that focus and level, right alongside the built-in content.

## Creating a custom drill

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Custom training content panel">
    In the Team tab, scroll to the **Custom training content** section. This panel is only visible to coaches.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Training Focus">
    Use the **Training Focus** dropdown to choose which focus area the drill belongs to — for example, Pitching Mechanics, Hitting, Fielding, or any other available focus in the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Skill Level">
    Use the **Skill Level** dropdown to pick the appropriate level for this drill. Matching the level accurately ensures the drill shows up for athletes working at that progression.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the drill name">
    Type a clear, descriptive name into the **Drill name** field. This name appears as the drill's heading on the training card and in athlete progress history, so make it recognizable.

    <Note>
      The drill name is required. You cannot save a custom drill without it.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add sets/reps (optional)">
    If your drill has a volume prescription, enter it in the **Sets/reps** field using a format like `2x/week, 10 reps` or `3 sets, 8 reps each side`. This field is optional — leave it blank if the drill is open-ended or time-based.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the drill steps">
    In the **Steps** field, enter each step of the drill on its own line. Steps are the sequential instructions athletes follow to perform the drill correctly. At least one step is required before the drill can be saved.

    **Example:**
    ```
    Start in your athletic stance at the edge of the infield dirt.
    Field the ground ball with two hands, staying low through the ball.
    Crow-hop and throw to first with a four-seam grip.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a self-review checklist (optional)">
    In the **Self-review checklist** field, enter each checklist item on its own line. These are the technique cues or quality markers athletes check off after completing the drill — they form the basis of the progress history you review in the Team tab.

    **Example:**
    ```
    Stayed low through the ball
    Used two hands on the catch
    Hit my target on the throw
    ```

    This field is optional, but adding a thoughtful checklist gives athletes a structured way to self-evaluate and gives you richer data when reviewing their history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the drill">
    Tap **Add Drill** (or your app's save button for the panel) to publish the drill. It will appear immediately on the relevant training card for that Focus + Level combination.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where custom drills appear

Once saved, your custom drills appear in a **"Coach's additions"** section at the bottom of the relevant training card. Every athlete who navigates to that Training Focus + Skill Level combination will see your drills alongside the standard content — no action required on their part.

<Tip>
  Use custom drills to introduce team-specific terminology, reinforce techniques you're emphasizing this season, or fill in content for positions or levels where you want more volume.
</Tip>

## Removing a custom drill

To remove a drill you've added:

1. Scroll to the **Custom training content** panel in the Team tab.
2. Find the drill in the custom drill list under its Focus + Level.
3. Click the **×** button next to the drill name.

The drill is removed immediately from all training cards. Athletes who had already checked it off in their history will retain that history, but the drill will no longer appear going forward.

<Warning>
  Removing a custom drill cannot be undone from within the app. If you want to preserve the drill's content, copy the steps and checklist before deleting it.
</Warning>

## Storage and portability

Custom drills are saved to this device's local storage. They also travel with your **team export file** — when you export your team data and an athlete imports that file, they receive all of your custom drills as part of the package. This means your entire team sees consistent content even if they're using different devices.
