Player Forged lets you push specific training assignments directly to each athlete so they know exactly what to work on when they're training on their own. Assignments are designed for **non-practice days** — they fill the gaps between team sessions and complement whatever is already on the Practice Calendar. You'll find the assignment controls inside each athlete's panel in the Team tab.

## Two ways to assign training

### Method 1 — Suggested chips

Suggested chips are automatically generated for each athlete based on two factors: the positions you tagged them with when adding them to the roster, and which Training Focuses have already been covered by the team this week on the Practice Calendar. The app surfaces focuses that are relevant to that athlete's positions *and* that haven't yet been addressed in a scheduled team practice — so their home training fills real gaps rather than duplicating work.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Expand the athlete's panel">
    In the Team tab, tap an athlete's name to expand their assignment panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the suggested chips">
    A row of suggested chips appears near the top of the panel. Each chip represents a Training Focus + Skill Level pairing that the app recommends for that athlete this week.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a chip to assign it">
    Tap any chip to instantly add that Focus + Level combination as an assignment for the athlete. The chip moves into the active assignments list below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Suggested chips refresh automatically each week as the Practice Calendar updates and athletes check off completed work. Check back at the start of each week to see new recommendations.
</Tip>

### Method 2 — Manual assignment

If you want to assign something specific that isn't surfaced as a suggestion — perhaps a focus you're emphasizing this season or a skill level adjustment for an individual player — you can build any assignment manually.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the manual assignment controls">
    Inside the athlete's panel, locate the **Training Focus** and **Skill Level** dropdowns beneath the suggested chips section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Training Focus">
    Select any focus from the **Training Focus** dropdown. All available focuses in the app are listed here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Skill Level">
    Select the appropriate level from the **Skill Level** dropdown. Levels range from beginner through advanced.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the assignment">
    Tap **Assign** to add that Focus + Level combination to the athlete's active assignments. You can repeat this process to stack multiple assignments for the same athlete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each athlete can hold multiple active assignments at the same time. There's no cap — assign as many Focus + Level combinations as makes sense for their training plan.
</Note>

## Assignment status

Every assignment in an athlete's list shows one of two statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Done this week** | The athlete tapped the checkbox on their end and marked this assignment complete |
| **Not done yet** | The assignment is active and the athlete hasn't checked it off yet |

Statuses reset automatically at the start of each new week, so you get a clean slate every Monday without any manual cleanup.

## What athletes see

Once you've added assignments, athletes will see a banner labeled **"Assigned by your coach"** at the top of their Training tab. Each assignment appears as a row in that banner with two actions:

- **Go** — jumps them directly into the training card for that Focus + Level combination so they can start immediately
- **Checkbox** — lets them mark the assignment as done once they've completed it

Athletes do not see the Team tab or roster management tools — they only see their own assigned work in the Training tab banner.
