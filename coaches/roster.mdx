The Team tab is your coaching hub in Player Forged — it's where you build your roster, review progress, assign training, and customize your team's experience. Only coaches have full access to the Team tab. Athletes who open the app in the Player role see their personal training view but cannot access roster management, assignment tools, or branding settings. Everything on this page assumes you are using the app in the **Coach** role.

## Adding an athlete

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team tab">
    Tap **Team** in the bottom navigation bar. The roster panel appears at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the athlete's name">
    Type the athlete's full name (or nickname — whatever you'd like displayed throughout the app) into the **Athlete name** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select one or more positions">
    Choose at least one position from the available options:

    - **Pitcher**
    - **Catcher**
    - **Infielder**
    - **Outfielder**
    - **Hitter**
    - **Base Running**

    You can select multiple positions for athletes who play more than one role. Position tags appear as toggleable chips — tap a chip to select it, tap again to deselect.

    <Note>
      At least one position must be selected before you can add the athlete. The **Add Athlete** button stays inactive until a position is chosen.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Athlete">
    Tap **Add Athlete** to save the player to your roster. They will appear immediately in the roster list, and their positions will be used to generate suggested training assignments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Position selections directly influence the auto-suggested training chips in the assignment panel. The more accurately you tag each athlete's roles, the more relevant their suggested drills will be.
</Tip>

## Removing an athlete

To remove a player from your roster, locate their name in the roster list and click the **remove** (×) button next to their entry. A confirmation prompt will appear before the deletion is finalized.

<Warning>
  Removing an athlete permanently deletes all of their training assignments from the app on this device. Their historical progress data (imported from their training file) is not recoverable from within the app once the athlete is removed. Export your team file before removing athletes if you want to preserve a record.
</Warning>

## What the roster shows

Each athlete entry in the roster displays a summary of their activity at a glance:

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Positions** | The position tags assigned when the athlete was added |
| **Completed drills** | Total number of drills the athlete has checked off across all time |
| **Stickers** | Total stickers the athlete has earned through consistent training |

This summary gives you a quick read on each player's engagement before you dive into their individual progress panel below.

<Note>
  The Team tab is only fully accessible to coaches. Athletes using the Player role see their personal training view and cannot access roster management, assignment tools, or branding settings.
</Note>
